About Zohobkmail

Zohobkmail is a small publishing tool built for creators who finish videos on a computer and want to post them to TikTok and YouTube without juggling phones, transfers and re-typed captions.

It started as an internal tool for a short-form studio publishing several videos a day across channels, and is now offered to other creators. The product uses only official platform APIs and follows each platform's developer terms.

Principles

  • Official sign-in only — never passwords.
  • Local first — your tokens and files stay on your computer.
  • Your rules — privacy level, comments, duet, stitch and disclosure are always your choice, per post.
