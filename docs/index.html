Connecting TikTok

Zohobkmail uses TikTok Login Kit. Press Connect TikTok account; TikTok opens its authorization page and shows the requested permissions:

ScopeWhy
user.info.basicShow the connected account's name and avatar in the app.
video.publishPost the video you chose to your profile (Direct Post).

After approval TikTok redirects to https://zohobkmail.com/callback/, which hands the authorization code back to the app running on your computer. The code is exchanged for a token locally; nothing is stored on this website.

Posting options

OptionValues
CaptionText, #hashtags, @mentions · TikTok length limit shown live
Who can viewPrivacy levels allowed for your account (queried from TikTok)
Comments / Duet / StitchOn or off, each
Content disclosureOff · Your brand · Branded content
ScheduleNow, or a date and time

Video requirements

  • MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio) · vertical 9:16 recommended.
  • Length and file size follow the limits TikTok reports for your account (the app checks before posting).

Status and errors

Posts move through queued → uploading → processing → posted. If TikTok rejects a post, the reason is shown next to it (for example an unsupported privacy level or a video that is too long). Fix the post and retry; nothing is published twice.

Disconnecting and data

  • Disconnect in the app deletes the token from your computer.
  • Revoke from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → Zohobkmail.
  • What we store and for how long: see the Privacy Policy.
