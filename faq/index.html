Frequently asked questions

Is Zohobkmail an official TikTok or YouTube product?

No. Zohobkmail is an independent tool that uses the official TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API. TikTok and YouTube are trademarks of their owners.

Do you need my TikTok password?

No. You sign in on TikTok's own page (Login Kit). Zohobkmail only receives a token you can revoke at any time.

Where are my videos and tokens stored?

On your computer. The app runs locally and sends the video only to the platform you publish to. This website stores nothing.

Can I choose who sees the video?

Yes. The app shows the privacy levels your TikTok account allows and you pick one for each post. You also control comments, duet and stitch.

Can I schedule posts?

Yes. Set a date and time; the app posts automatically and shows the result.

Why did my post fail?

The app shows TikTok's reason next to the post — usually video length, size, or a privacy level not available to the account. Fix it and retry.

How do I disconnect?

Press Disconnect in the app, or revoke Zohobkmail under TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

How do I contact you?

E-mail contact@zohobkmail.com or use the support page.
