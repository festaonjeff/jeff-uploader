Features

Everything you need to get a finished short video from your computer onto your channels, and nothing you don't.

Accounts

  • TikTok Login Kit — connect your TikTok account with the official sign-in. Scopes requested: user.info.basic (show which account is connected) and video.publish (post videos). You can disconnect from the app or from TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • YouTube — connect a channel with Google sign-in to publish Shorts and regular videos.
  • Connect several accounts and pick the destination per post.

Publishing to TikTok (Content Posting API · Direct Post)

  • Caption with hashtags and mentions (up to the TikTok limit shown live).
  • Who can view this video: the exact privacy levels your account allows (Public, Friends, Only me…), fetched from TikTok each time.
  • Interactions: allow comments, duet and stitch — each on or off.
  • Content disclosure: mark Your brand / Branded content when the post is promotional, as required by TikTok.
  • Publishing status is polled and shown until TikTok reports the post as live, with a link to open it.

Scheduling

  • Pick a date and time; the app posts automatically.
  • Queue view with upcoming, posted and failed items; failed posts explain why.

Privacy by design

  • Runs on your own computer. Access tokens, captions and video files never leave it except to the platform you publish to.
  • No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party sharing. See the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

  • macOS 13+ or Windows 10+, a modern browser, and a TikTok or YouTube account you own.
  • Videos: MP4 (H.264/AAC), vertical 9:16 recommended, up to the size and length limits of each platform.
