How it works

1. Connect your account

Open the app and press Connect TikTok account. TikTok shows its own authorization screen listing the permissions Zohobkmail asks for. After you approve, you return to the app and see your username and avatar.

2. Prepare the post

Choose a finished video file. Write the caption, then choose Who can view this video, whether to allow comments, duet and stitch, and whether to disclose branded content. The app shows the limits of your account (video length, privacy options) before you post.

3. Publish or schedule

Press Publish now, or set a time and press Schedule. The video is uploaded to TikTok through the Content Posting API and the app shows the publishing status until it is live.

4. Check status

The dashboard lists every post with its state (queued, uploading, processing, posted, failed) and a link to the post. Failed posts show TikTok's reason so you can fix and retry.

Disconnecting

Press Disconnect in the app to remove the token from your computer, or revoke Zohobkmail from TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
