Zohobkmail

Zohobkmail is a video publishing tool for short-form creators. Connect your YouTube and TikTok accounts with the official login, pick a finished video, write the caption, choose privacy settings, and publish or schedule it — from one place.

How it works

  1. Sign in to TikTok with Login Kit and authorize Zohobkmail.
  2. Choose a video, caption, privacy level and interaction settings (comments, duet, stitch).
  3. Zohobkmail posts it to your TikTok profile through the Content Posting API and shows the publishing status.

You can disconnect at any time from the app or from TikTok settings.
