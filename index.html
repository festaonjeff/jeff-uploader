Official sign-in only

You authorize Zohobkmail through TikTok Login Kit and Google sign-in. We never ask for your password and you can revoke access any time.

Direct Post, your rules

Choose the privacy level, allow or block comments, duet and stitch, and mark branded content — exactly the options TikTok offers in the app.

Schedule a queue

Set a time and let the app post for you. Posts go out in order and you see the status of each one on the dashboard.

Same video, both platforms

Publish one finished file to TikTok and YouTube Shorts with separate captions and privacy settings for each.

Local-first privacy

Tokens, captions and files are kept on your computer. Zohobkmail has no cloud account and does not store your media.

Clear status, no surprises

Every post shows its publishing state and a link to the post in TikTok Studio or YouTube Studio once it is live.

How it works

  1. Connect. Sign in to TikTok with Login Kit and authorize Zohobkmail. The app receives only the permissions needed to publish and read status.
  2. Prepare. Pick a finished video, write a caption and hashtags, and choose privacy level and interaction settings.
  3. Publish. Zohobkmail posts it to your TikTok profile through the Content Posting API — now or at the scheduled time — and shows the result.
