Privacy Policy

Zohobkmail · Effective 2026-11-02

What this app is

Zohobkmail (zohobkmail.com) is a video publishing tool for creators. It runs on the user's own computer and publishes videos the user chooses to the user's own connected accounts (YouTube, TikTok).

Data we access

When an account is connected, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (account ID, username, display name, avatar) from the platform, and only the permissions needed to publish videos and read their status (for example TikTok user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

How data is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the user's local computer. Zohobkmail does not send them to any server of its own. Videos, titles, descriptions and hashtags are sent only to the platform the user chose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. We do not use data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its token from the local computer. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings (TikTok: Settings › Security › Manage app permissions). To request deletion of any data, email contact@zohobkmail.com.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page.
