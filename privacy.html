Privacy Policy

Jeff Uploader · Effective 2026-10-25

What this app is

Jeff Uploader is a desktop application run by its operator on the operator's own computer. It uploads videos the operator produced to social media accounts the operator owns or manages (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads). It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

When an account is connected, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (account ID, username, display name, avatar) from the platform, and only the permissions needed to publish videos and read their status (for example TikTok user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish; Instagram/Threads content publishing).

How data is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the operator's local computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by us; we do not run one. Videos, titles, descriptions and hashtags are sent only to the platform the operator chose.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. We do not use data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its token from the local computer. Access can also be revoked at any time in the platform's own settings (TikTok: Settings › Security › Manage app permissions; Instagram/Facebook: Settings › Apps and websites). To request deletion of any data, contact the operator listed in the platform's app registration.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page.
